How to play & rules

Controls

ArrowsSwim A / SpacePass (aim with direction) · on defense: switch swimmer · teammate has ball: call for it S holdShoot: charge and release (up/down picks the corner, a quick tap lobs) · on defense: tackle D / ShiftSprint. Every swimmer has an energy bar: sprinting drains it, a tired swimmer swims slower until it refills Esc / PPause 🧤 Mega goalieAfter a goal, the goalie who got scored on turns MEGA for 10 seconds: bigger, and about 35% more saves. Pass it around before you shoot! 🫧 Power-upsSwim through a floating bubble to grab Turbo, Mega Ball, Freeze, Duck Attack or Octopus Arms for your team. ⚡ Super shotPasses, steals and goalie saves charge the pink meter. When it's full, your next shot from the other team's half is a torpedo that's very hard to stop. Closer is better!

On-screen buttons change with the ball: PASS, SHOOT and SPRINT on offense; TACKLE, SWITCH and SPRINT on defense. Tap or click them, or use the keys. Gamepads work too.

Rules used (World Aquatics, current)

  • 5 field players + a goalie per side, 4 quarters, swim-off at the start of each quarter. Teams switch ends at halftime.
  • 28-second possession clock, reset to 18 after an offensive rebound, an exclusion or a corner.
  • Tackle from the front. Grabbing from behind is an ordinary foul (free throw). Holding a swimmer who doesn't have the ball is an exclusion: 18 seconds in the re-entry corner.
  • A foul from behind inside 5 m, when the shooter is facing goal, is a penalty from the 5 m line.
  • Free throw inside 6 m must be passed. Outside 6 m you may shoot straight away.
  • 2-meter rule: attackers without the ball can't wait inside the opponent's 2 m line.
  • Ball off a defender over the goal line is a corner; off an attacker, a goal throw. Three personal fouls and a player is replaced.
  • Tied after four quarters? Five-shooter penalty shootout, then sudden death.
  • Learn mode explains each rule the first time the ref calls it, labels positions, gives coach tips and asks a quick quiz question between quarters.
